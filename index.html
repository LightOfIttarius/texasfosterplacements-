Texas Foster Placements Transparency Dashboard

An independent analysis of publicly available data. Not a state rating.
How to read this: The safety score runs from 0 to 100 and compares each open general residential operation with the others in Texas. A higher score means fewer weighted inspection findings per inspection. It does not say whether a facility is safe in an absolute sense. Facilities with few inspections have low confidence and their scores can change a lot. A red flag means the state has recorded a corrective or adverse action. No information about individual children is collected or shown.
