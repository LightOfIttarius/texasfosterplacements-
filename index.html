Texas Foster Placements Transparency Dashboard

An independent analysis of publicly available data. Not a state rating.
How to read this: Right now each placement has a safety score only, based on state inspection findings. It runs from 0 to 100 and compares each open general residential operation with the others in Texas. A higher score means fewer weighted findings per inspection. It does not say whether a facility is safe in an absolute sense, and it is not an overall rating. Many things families care about are not published anywhere yet, and each card lists them as Not reported instead of guessing. Facilities with few inspections have low confidence. A red flag means the state has recorded a corrective or adverse action. No information about individual children is collected or shown.
