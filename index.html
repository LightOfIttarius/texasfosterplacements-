How to read these scores (tap to open)

The safety score

The safety score runs from 0 to 100. It compares each open general residential operation in Texas with the others, using state inspection findings. A higher score means fewer weighted findings per inspection compared with other Texas facilities. The score is relative.

The Inspection record rating

Facilities with a score also get a plain rating of Good, Average, or Poor. A facility with a state corrective or adverse action on record can never be rated Good. The rating is based on state inspection findings only.

Who gets a rating

Only facilities with 5 or more inspections get a score and a rating. Facilities with fewer show a preliminary Early signs note instead.

What is not in the score

Staffing ratios, turnover, discipline practices, food quality, education, therapy, and daily life experiences are not published by the state, so they appear as “Not reported.”

Limits

  • Inspection history covers an unknown time period.
  • Recent violations may be missing until due process is complete.
  • This is independent analysis and is not endorsed by the State of Texas.
